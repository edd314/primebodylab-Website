About

Fitness and lifestyle content creator based in Bavaria, Germany — and, unusually for the niche, also a certified personal trainer, sports massage therapist, and assisted-stretch specialist (founder of PrimeBodyLab). Content sits at the intersection of real training, the recovery side nobody else in the niche covers with actual credentials, and the honest mindset shifts that come with training past your twenties.

Content Pillars

  • Training — real sessions, real form, no filler
  • Recovery — the credentialed angle most fitness creators can't speak to
  • Mindset — relatable, specific, not generic motivation
  • Lifestyle — the person behind the training, not just the gym

Why This Fits Your Brand

  • Credentialed voice — not just an opinion, an actual practitioner
  • Consistent visual identity, tight niche, no follower-count padding
