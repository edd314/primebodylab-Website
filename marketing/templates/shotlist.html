Editing recipe

  1. Pick audio with a sharp, clear beat first — the format lives or dies on the music.
  2. Drop 10–14 clips on the timeline in your chosen order.
  3. Trim each clip to 1.5–2s, snapped so the cut lands on a beat/snare hit.
  4. Hard cuts only — no fades, no slide transitions.
  5. Text overlay on the first clip (hook) and the last clip (brand tag/CTA) only.
  6. End on one extra beat: brand cream background + wordmark, ~1s.

Shot bank — check off as you film

Fitness
  • Wide establishing shot — walking in
  • Mid-rep freeze — hardest moment of a set
  • Close-up on hands/grip — chalk, bar, band
  • Post-set exhale / wiping down
  • Walking shot — entrance or exit
Fashion
  • Full-body static outfit shot
  • Detail close-up — shoes, watch, logo tee
  • Candid adjust — collar, jacket, sleeve
  • Walk-away or turn shot
  • Mirror / reflection shot
Brand
  • Branded item close-up — logo tee, signage
  • Treatment room / equipment detail
  • Solo close-up, direct to camera

The recycling trick: one "gym day" + one "fashion day" fills the whole bank. Every reel after that is just a new 10–14 pick, new order, new song — until the bank feels stale and it's time to refresh it.
