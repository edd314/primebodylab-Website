## Concept: "It Adds Up" → "Sore Today. Unstoppable Tomorrow."

**Psychology:** Nike/Apple-style arc — struggle → transformation → identity, not a feature pitch. PrimeBodyLab enters quietly as the turning point, not the headline. Ends on identity ("unstoppable"), not a service list.

---

### ACT 1 — THE STRUGGLE (0-8s)
**Visual:** Quick hard-cut montage, cool/gray-toned light, real textures — stiff shoulder roll after a desk day, wincing getting off the couch, an athlete finishing a hard session and stretching a tight hamstring, rubbing a sore neck at a red light. No dialogue yet, no Eddie yet — universal, not brand-specific.
**VO (Eddie, low, warm, almost thinking out loud):** "Every rep. Every long day. Every mile. It adds up."
**On-screen text:** none — let it breathe.

### ACT 2 — THE TURN (8-16s)
**Visual:** Tone shifts warmer. A candle lighting, a towel unfolding, the treatment room door easing open into golden light. Cut to Eddie's hands beginning real work — assisted stretch, deep tissue, focused expression. Client's shoulders visibly drop, an audible exhale.
**VO:** "This is where it stops adding up."
**On-screen text:** none.

### ACT 3 — THE TRANSFORMATION (16-24s)
**Visual:** Contrast payoff — the same tightness from Act 1, now released. Shoulder rolling free and easy, someone walking out into daylight looser, a runner picking pace back up, a lifter racking a bar with no hesitation.
**VO:** "Recovery isn't rest. It's how you keep going."

### ACT 4 — IDENTITY / PAYOFF (24-30s)
**Visual:** Eddie, direct to camera, calm and grounded, treatment room behind him.
**VO (statement, not a pitch):** "Sore today. Unstoppable tomorrow."
**On-screen text (end card):** PrimeBodyLab — Massage & Sports Recovery · Book your first session
**Caption line (bilingual, small):** Heute Muskelkater. Morgen unaufhaltbar.

## Scaling for shorter cuts
